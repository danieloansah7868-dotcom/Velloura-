Privacy policy

This page explains what personal information VELLOURA collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

When you place an order we collect your name, phone number, delivery area, neighborhood and any note you choose to send us.

How we use it

We use your details to confirm your order on WhatsApp, arrange delivery, and answer any questions before or after purchase. We do not sell your information.

Payment

We do not store bank or card details. Mobile Money is collected by the owner directly after order confirmation.

Messages on WhatsApp

When you tap the WhatsApp button we open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. WhatsApp is a separate service and its own privacy terms apply.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your details at any time. Message us on WhatsApp and we will help.
