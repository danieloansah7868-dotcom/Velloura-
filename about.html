About Velloura

VELLOURA is a Ghanaian women's brand built on one simple idea: every woman deserves to feel royal, feminine and confident when she steps out.

We sell clothing and jewelry that is easy to wear, made with care, and priced for real life. We also stock hair and wigs that help you look your best without the stress.

We deliver nationwide, we accept Mobile Money transfer on order confirmation, and we can send fitting photos before you say yes.

Velloura is proud to be made in Ghana, for Ghanaian women.
