Delivery and returns

Delivery

VELLOURA delivers nationwide in Ghana.

  • Accra: GHS 20
  • Kumasi: GHS 30
  • Other regions: GHS 40

Delivery is free for orders of GHS 500 or more.

How delivery works

After you place an order, VELLOURA confirms it on WhatsApp. We agree on a delivery day and time, then send the package out.

Fitting photos

We can send fitting photos of clothing before you confirm, so you can check the look or style first.

Returns

We accept returns within 3 days for items that have not been worn, washed or damaged. Thrifts and one-of-one items are sold as found and can only be returned if faulty. Message us on WhatsApp before sending anything back.
